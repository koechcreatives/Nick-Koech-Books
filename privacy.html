Privacy policy

Last updated: October 2026

This site (nickkoechbooks.site) is run by Nick Koech. This page explains what information we collect and how we use it.

What we collect

Your email address and password when you create an account. Your password is stored securely and I cannot see it. A record of the books you buy, including the amount and payment reference. An identifier for the phone or browser you read on, which we use to keep your account linked to your device. If you join through a referral link, the code of the person who invited you. Where you stop reading is saved in your own browser.

Payments

Payments are handled by Paystack. Your card or mobile money details go to Paystack and are not stored on this site.

How we use it

To run your account, let you read the books you bought, confirm payments, help you if something goes wrong, and pay referral commission. We do not sell your information.

Who else sees it

Our service providers only: Supabase (account and purchase records), Paystack (payments) and our email sender (account and password emails).

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete your account information at any time by emailing nickkoechbooks@gmail.com.
